# Historical training and benchmarks

These stage-specific recipes cover Stages 4–10, including Stage 5 Small
and the backgame and Paskogammon training experiments.
Their previous paths under `scripts/` are compatibility symlinks. Use either
path with a source checkout and its local engine build. Historical recipes
may require datasets or checkpoints from their original experiments.

For current training, see the [training pipeline](../../docs/training.md).
